## 3. The mechanics everyone in this niche shares

Write the 9 that actually apply. Example shape (from a real audit):

1. One subject, one thesis — never a listicle.
2. Superlative + contradiction in the title.
3. Numeric specificity — numbers read as evidence.
4. An injustice engine — a villain, a victim, a thing that was taken.
5. Authority through specificity — names, dates, documents, sources.
6. Identity binding — the viewer is the subject.
7. An open loop in the first 15 seconds, closed at the end.
8. Serialisation where possible.
9. A second business behind the channel.

## 5. Format spec (do not deviate)

- **Clip limit:** Omni 10 s · Veo 8 s · this project uses `__` s

  - `HOOK` — the interrupt, no logo, no "hey guys"
  - `SETUP` — identity, stakes
  - `TURN` — the mechanism
