# Post Anatomy — the one format every project uses

> **The engine.** This is the canonical post block. `tools/new-post.js` generates
> it and `tools/validate-posts.js` parses it. Do not change the field names —
> change the values.

A post is **one production record**: hook, prompt table, caption, comments and
CTA in one block. Never split a post across files.

---

## 1. The canonical block

Copy this into `03_POST_BANK.md`, one after another.

```markdown
## POST 01 — "Title Of The Post"
**Pillar:** [pillar name]
**Interrupt:** [01–12] — [category name]
**Psychographic:** [who this is for, in one line]
**Hook:** "[the first spoken line]"
**Length:** [NN]s
**Model:** [Omni|Veo]

**Clips:**

| # | Time | Dur | Visual | Overlay | Narration |
|---|------|-----|--------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | 0:00–0:06 | 6s | [full six-part prompt + negative] | [text or —] | [line] |
| 2 | 0:06–0:12 | 6s | [...] | [text or —] | [line] |
| 3 | 0:12–0:18 | 6s | [...] | [text or —] | [line] |
| 4 | 0:18–0:24 | 6s | [...] | [text or —] | [line] |

**Post Text:** [the caption body, 1–4 short lines]

**Comments:**
1. [engagement comment]
2. [engagement comment]
3. [engagement comment]

**CTA:** [one line, one destination]
```

---

## 2. Field rules

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| `## POST NN — "Title"` | NN is zero-padded and unique. Title is the working title, not necessarily the published one. |
| `Pillar` | Must exist in `01_PILLARS.md`. |
| `Interrupt` | `NN — Name` from `_SHARED/interrupt-taxonomy.md`. Must differ from the previous post. |
| `Psychographic` | One line: who feels this, and what they fear or want. |
| `Hook` | The first spoken line. Must be the interrupt, not a setup. |
| `Length` | **Content** runtime in seconds. The published runtime adds the project intro (≤1.5 s) and outro (1.5–3 s) — see `_SHARED/intro-outro.md`. |
| `Model` | `Veo` (max 8 s/clip) or `Omni` (max 10 s/clip). Default `Veo`. |
| `Clips` table | One row per generated clip. Times are `m:ss–m:ss`. `Dur` is `Ns`. |
| `Visual` | The full prompt: six parts + negative prompt, in one cell. Use `<br>` if you need line breaks inside the cell. |
| `Overlay` | On-screen text, added in the editor — **never generated by the model**. `—` if none. |
| `Narration` | The spoken or narrated line for that clip. `—` if music only. |
| `Post Text` | The caption body. First line must be the hook, rewritten for the platform. |
| `Comments` | Exactly three, each a real question or a real observation. No "nice post". |
| `CTA` | One line, one destination. Same line is spoken in the final clip. |

## 3. Clip table patterns

**Social post** (4–5 clips, 22–35 s):

| # | Job | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | GRAB — the interrupt | 4–6 s |
| 2 | LABEL — category / teaching line | 4–6 s |
| 3 | TEACH — the mechanism | 5–6 s |
| 4 | PROOF — the receipt | 5–6 s |
| 5 | CTA — one line | 4–6 s |

**Story post** (6–8 clips, 40–60 s):

| # | Job | Typical duration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | HOOK — interrupt + claim | 6–8 s |
| 2 | SETUP — identity, stakes | 6–8 s |
| 3 | TURN — the mechanism | 6–8 s |
| 4 | TURN 2 — escalation | 6–8 s |
| 5 | PAYOFF — deliver the promise | 6–8 s |
| 6 | CLOSE — meaning + CTA | 6–8 s |

**Proof clips** are real screenshots framed in the editor. Their `Visual` cell
reads `REAL SCREENSHOT — [what it shows]. Do not generate.`

---

## 4. Writing the Visual cell

One cell, six parts, then the negatives. This is the part that becomes a prompt.

```
[1 Camera: locked camera, no zoom pan tilt or rotation, 9:16 vertical.]
[2 Subject: ...] [3 Effect: ... verb + direction ...]
[4 Environment & light: ...] [5 Style & palette: ...]
NEGATIVE: ...
```

Keep it under ~120 words per clip so it stays paste-ready.

## 5. Two proven renderings

The same anatomy expresses both styles. Choose by project, not by habit.

**A. Scene-style** (documentary / biology / factual) — each clip is one
irreversible action, narrated in second person, ending unresolved.

```markdown
## POST 07 — "Six Needles"
**Pillar:** Hidden processes
**Interrupt:** 02 — Scale & Proportion
**Psychographic:** Anyone who has been bitten and never thought about it.
**Hook:** "You've been stabbed six times."
**Length:** 32s
**Model:** Veo

**Clips:**

| # | Time | Dur | Visual | Overlay | Narration |
|---|------|-----|--------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | 0:00–0:06 | 6s | Hyper-macro 5cm from skin like cracked desert terrain, a mosquito leg touches down, golden backlight raking from the left, needle-tip like a blade. Locked camera, 9:16. NEGATIVE: strings, visible hand, camera drift, text, watermark. | You've been stabbed six times | You've been stabbed six times. You didn't feel a thing. |
| 2 | 0:06–0:12 | 6s | Cross-section under the skin, six stylets working in sequence — two sawing, two clamping tissue open, one gliding in numbing fluid. Locked camera, 9:16. NEGATIVE: camera drift, cartoon, text. | — | Two saws cut. Two clamps hold you open. One needle numbs you. |
```

**B. Story-style** (human / service / cause) — each clip advances an emotional
arc, one character, continuity clause repeated.

```markdown
## POST 11 — "The Empty Chair"
**Pillar:** The hidden story
**Interrupt:** 05 — Material & Texture
**Psychographic:** Parents who fear missing warning signs.
**Hook:** "She didn't drop out. Something happened."
**Length:** 48s
**Model:** Veo

**Clips:**

| # | Time | Dur | Visual | Overlay | Narration |
|---|------|-----|--------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | 0:00–0:08 | 8s | An empty wooden school chair in a bright classroom; the neatly folded school uniform on it dissolves into loose paper that blows upward while the room stays still. Locked camera, 9:16. NEGATIVE: camera drift, people, text, watermark. | She didn't drop out | She didn't drop out. Something happened. |
| 2 | 0:08–0:16 | 8s | The empty chair fills with notebooks, then the notebooks disappear one by one, leaving the chair bare. Locked camera, 9:16. NEGATIVE: camera drift, hands, text. | — | Sometimes a girl stops going to school because the problem isn't inside the classroom. |
```

Both are valid. A project may use one style throughout, or mix by pillar — but
the **field names never change**, so the validator and the tools always work.

## 6. Post checklist

- [ ] Header, all six fields, clips table, post text, three comments, CTA
- [ ] First clip contains the interrupt inside 1–2 s
- [ ] Every clip within the model limit
- [ ] Every visual states the effect as verb + direction
- [ ] Every visual includes a negative prompt
- [ ] No text is requested from the model
- [ ] Character continuity clause pasted on every returning character
- [ ] Payoff clip delivers what the hook promised
- [ ] Category differs from the previous post
- [ ] Post text's first line is the hook, not context
- [ ] Three comments are real questions or observations
- [ ] CTA is single, soft, specific
